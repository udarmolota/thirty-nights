# 30 Nights — Concept Art Brief (two screens)

We need concept art for a mobile survival-strategy game. Two screens, one
consistent look. This brief is written for a concept artist or for an
image-generation model; a prompt-ready summary is at the end.

## The game in one breath

Two people missed the last evacuation flight out of a remote arctic mining
village. They have two weeks of shrinking daylight to chop wood, saw planks,
board up the fence and loot the abandoned houses for food — and then survive
thirty nights of polar darkness while wolves, and something worse, circle the
perimeter. Grounded, quiet, cold. Not cute, not cartoon, not gore, not fantasy.

**Era:** mid-20th-century mining settlement, generator dead: wood stoves, oil
lamps, plank fences, log-and-plank buildings, tin roofs under snow.
**Real reference:** Svalbard coal settlements (Barentsburg, Longyearbyen).

## Platform and format

- Android phone, **landscape**, safe frame 2400 × 1080 px (S25 Ultra class).
  (If a portrait version is requested: 1080 × 2400, same content, panels
  stacked top and bottom instead of at the sides.)
- Deliver PNG at 2400 × 1080, plus layered source (PSD/Procreate/Aseprite) if
  possible: UI panels on their own layers, map separate.

## Camera and readability — the rules that never change

- **Top-down, near-vertical camera** (Prison Architect, RimWorld, Final
  Outpost). NOT isometric, NOT 3/4, NOT side view. Walls read as **lines**
  seen from above; a broken wall is a **gap in the line**.
- **Tiny ground tiles**: a person is larger than a tile; a fence section is 4–6
  tiles long; damage shows as a few missing tiles in a line.
- **People are placeholders**: colored discs with an initial (blue "И" Ivan,
  green "М" Marta). If you want to propose a real top-down figure, do it as a
  separate small study — the discs must still work.
- Three perimeter states must be told apart at a glance: **intact**,
  **damaged / gap**, **reinforced (doubled)**. Also **missing** (never built):
  a ghost line.

## Palette — "Polar Night"

Cold world, warm light only where something burns.

Snow: `#F4F7FB` `#DFE7F2` `#B9C7DD` `#8FA2C2` (shadows are BLUE, never grey)
Night / outline: `#1B1D2E` `#2A2F4A`   Ice: `#7FB3D5` `#4F86B0`
Stone & metal: `#AEB4C2` `#9AA0AD` `#6E7482` `#474B58`
Raw wood: `#B07D4B` `#8A5A32` `#5D3B20` `#3A2415`   Planks: `#C9A97A` `#A3865C`
Spruce: `#2F5D45` `#1F4030` `#142A20`   Skin: `#F1C9A5` `#C8956C` `#8F5A3C`
Cloth: `#7A6A55` `#55483A` `#545A70` `#3B3F52`
**Fire — the only warm saturated hues:** `#FFE08A` `#FFB03A` `#E85D2A` `#9C2A1E`
Vampire accent: `#B8C4D6` `#7C8AA3`, eye `#FF2F4F`, blood `#7A1225`

Pixel art is welcome (32-px tile scale) but not required; a painted top-down
look in this palette is equally fine. What matters is that the two screens
belong to one hand.

## Screen 1 — The Base (day 6 of 14, mid-afternoon, low sun)

**Composition.** The map fills the screen; UI sits in a slim top bar and a
narrow side panel (right). A large, irregular **hangar / mine workshop** in the
middle (an L-shape or a main hall with a lean-to), log walls with snow caps,
tin roof under snow, big double **gate**. A **plank fence encloses the whole
compound** — the hangar AND its yard — with a gate in the fence facing the
village road. Trodden paths in the snow: gate → hangar door, hangar → woodpile,
gate → forest edge. Spruce forest with heavy snow along two edges of the map;
the village road leaves the map at a third edge. Snowdrifts against the north
walls.

**Inside the hangar (roof shown cut away, top-down) — three rooms of different
character, doors between them:**
- the **big workshop hall** with a row of **large windows** along one wall
  (bright, but it leaks heat): a **sawhorse** with a half-sawn log, a workbench,
  the **woodpile** and a stack of planks, the gate; one of the big windows is
  **boarded over** with crossed planks, another is **cracked**;
- the **small office** with one small window: two **beds** with a free tile
  beside each, a table — the only room worth heating;
- a **storeroom / lean-to**: crates, sacks, a dead lamp; unheated.

**Three wood stoves stand where the old chimneys are** — one in the hall, one
in the office, one in the lean-to. Only the **office stove is lit**: a warm
pool of light on the plank floor there; the other two are cold and dark. A
newly built **partition** wall cuts the hall's windowed half off from the rest
(planks, obviously new against the old logs). The unheated rooms read visibly
colder: dimmer, blue, frost on the glass.

**Perimeter states visible at once:** one **hole in the hangar's east wall**
(splintered gap, red marker); one **missing fence section** on the south side
(ghost line, "build"); one **reinforced fence section** by the gate (doubled
planks, extra posts). Everything else intact.

**People:** Ivan (blue disc "И") at the fence hole with a small progress bar
and a health tag "72%"; Marta (green disc "М") inside at the sawhorse.

**HUD, top bar (left → right):** Day 6/14 · 14:20 · a daylight bar labelled
"СВЕТ 5.8 ч" · counters with line icons: temperature "+2°", food "23 (11 дн)",
logs "48", planks "17", fuel "96" · speed controls: pause, ×1 (active), ×2, ×3.
**Side panel (right), the action sheet for the tapped fence section:**
title "Забор · пролом", two buttons "Починить · 4 доски · 2 ч" (primary) and
"Укрепить · 3 доски · 1.5 ч", then "КТО:" with two chips "Иван 1.2× · занят"
(dimmed) and "Марта 0.9×" (selected). UI copy is Russian.

**Mood.** Late polar afternoon: long blue shadows, the sun a low smear on the
horizon side, the hangar's stove-lit corner the only warm thing on screen.
Quiet, not dramatic. No enemies visible by day.

## Screen 2 — The Village (planning a run, same day)

**Composition.** A map of the settlement, stylised but readable as a place, not
a diagram. The base sits at one edge (bottom or left) with its fence. **One
main road** runs through the village, with **branch paths** to houses — no
straight rays from the base. Snow, spruce clumps, a frozen stream or the
mine's rail spur can add structure. Travel time is measured **along the road**,
so a house far down the main street can be closer than a nearby one at the end
of a branch.

**Houses by type**, each recognisable at a glance from above: **grocery store**
(big roof, sign), **timber warehouse** (long, open side, plank stacks),
**pharmacy** (small, a cross), **garage** (vehicle bay, a dead truck outside),
**hospital** (larger, a cross, an annex), and about **eight residential
houses** (small, varied). Two or three houses are **already looted**: darker,
door open, no label accent. Unlooted houses may show a faint lit window — a
hint that someone or something is inside.

**Labels:** house name and travel time ("Магазин · 2.5 ч", "жилой · 1 ч") in
small pills; a **selected route** to the grocery store highlighted along the
road with "6.0 ч · Иван".

**HUD:** same top bar (compact: "ВЫЛАЗКА · день 6 · 14:20", daylight bar). Side
panel: "Магазин · осталось 25 еды", two runner rows — "Иван · 6.0 ч · несёт 10"
(selected) and "Марта · 4.6 ч · несёт 7" — a warning line "вернётся за 0.2 ч до
темноты", and a primary button "Отправить Ивана".

**Mood.** Same afternoon light, seen wider: the village is empty and silent,
smoke from only one chimney — ours.

## Icons

Line icons, one stroke weight, no emoji: sun, thermometer, food, log, plank,
flame, pause/play, walking figure, backpack.

## What we do NOT want

Isometric or side views; cute/chibi characters; zombie-apocalypse clichés;
neon or magenta lighting; gradients-as-style; gore; medieval fantasy;
sci-fi; text in the map other than the labels named above.

## Deliverables and process

1. **Two concept frames** (base, village) in one style — or, if exploring,
   three thumbnails of the base screen first, then the two frames in the
   chosen style.
2. After approval: a **style sheet** — tile size, outline rule, shading rule,
   the palette actually used — so production sprites (tiles, fence states,
   props, house types) can follow in the same hand. Our internal constants:
   32-px tile grid, 1-px `#1B1D2E` outline, hard-edged shading, max 3 tones per
   material, no baked shadows or glow (the engine draws light and shadow).

## Prompt-ready summary (for an image model)

Top-down concept art, near-vertical camera like Prison Architect, of a small
fortified compound in an arctic mining village at late polar afternoon: an
irregular log hangar with a snow-covered tin roof and a big gate, a plank
fence enclosing the whole compound including its yard, trodden paths in deep
snow, heavy-snow spruce forest along two edges, a road leaving toward the
village. Roof cut away to show three rooms: a big workshop hall with a row of
large windows (one boarded over) holding a sawhorse and a woodpile, a small
office with two beds, a storeroom; three wood stoves at the old chimneys, only
the office stove lit with a warm pool of light on the plank floor, the other
rooms dim and blue with frost on the glass; a new plank partition cutting off
the hall's windowed half. One splintered hole in the east wall, one missing
fence section drawn as a ghost line, one doubled reinforced section by the
gate. Two people as colored discs with initials. Slim dark HUD bar on top with counters and speed buttons, a
narrow action panel on the right. Cold palette of blue-white snow and dark
navy shadows, warm orange light only from the stove. Grounded, quiet,
mid-20th-century, no electricity. Not isometric, not cartoon, no gore,
no neon. Landscape 2400×1080.

## Addendum — the "clean plate" (painted background for the engine)

The engine will use the base and the village as **static painted backgrounds**
with live layers drawn on top (people, damage, boards, fire light, smoke,
snowfall, night tint, highlights). For that the render must be a clean plate:

1. **No HUD, no labels, no text** — the interface is drawn by the engine.
2. **Everything intact and empty:** fence complete, windows whole, no boarded
   windows, stoves **unlit**, no smoke, no people, no snowfall. Damage, fire,
   smoke and snow are engine layers; a painted flame would burn even when the
   stove is out.
3. **Flat daylight or overcast**, no baked fire light or long dramatic shadows —
   night and light pools are added by the engine.
4. **One consistent camera tilt** across the whole image, so pieces cut from
   it (fence section, wall section, boarded window) tile back into it.
5. **Resolution:** 3000–4000 px on the long side, PNG.
6. **Base:** either the three-room hangar from Screen 1, or two buildings inside
   one fence — a small bunkhouse (beds + stove) and a big workshop (sawhorse,
   workbench, stove). Both work; the two-building layout reads faster.
7. **Village:** one main road with branch paths, forest ring, **12–15 small
   houses of visibly different types** — grocery with a sign, long timber
   warehouse, small pharmacy with a cross, garage with a dead truck, larger
   hospital, the rest residential and varied; **two or three roofless ruins**
   (the "looted" look). Parchment-map treatment is welcome for this screen.

Separate small deliverables after the plates: one **intact fence section**,
one **broken** and one **reinforced** variant, one **wall section**, one
**boarded window**, one **plank partition piece** — cut from the same render so
they match its tilt and light.
